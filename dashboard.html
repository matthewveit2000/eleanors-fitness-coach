🎯 80/20 Routine Principles

  • Full body days: 3 core days hitting opposing muscle groups; far more time-efficient than bro-splits.
  • Antagonist supersets: Push ↔ Pull pairings share zero equipment and maximize rest between sets.
  • Inferred working weights: Based on 90% training-max of your estimated 1RM from real logged sets.
