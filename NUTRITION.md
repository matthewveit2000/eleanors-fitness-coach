## The Zero Food-Prep Protocol

Eleanor does **not** food prep. To hit 130g protein inside a tighter 1,500 kcal budget with zero tupperware prep, her plan relies on **high-yield, ready-to-eat convenience staples**:

### Preferred Staples (Instant & Grab-and-Go)
- **Fairlife Chocolate Protein Shakes (Ready-to-Drink):** 150 kcal, 30g protein, 2.5g fat, 4g carbs. (Perfect for fast hospital breakroom fueling).
- **Lactose-Free Milk (e.g. Fairlife 2% Ultra-Filtered):** 1 cup = 120 kcal, 13g protein, 4.5g fat, 6g carbs.
- **Eggs / Pre-Boiled Eggs:** 2 large eggs = ~140 kcal, 12g protein, 10g fat, 1g carbs.
- **Single-Serve Greek Yogurt / Skyr:** e.g. Oikos Triple Zero or Chobani Zero Sugar (100 kcal, 15g protein).
- **Rotisserie Chicken & Pre-Cooked Poultry:** Grab-and-go from the grocery deli (shred onto wraps, salads, or microwave with rice bowls).
- **Pre-Cooked Carb Packs:** Microwave brown rice, microwave quinoa pouches, frozen sweet potato bites, fruit (bananas, apples, berries).

## Shift-Work Nutrition Strategy (6 ON / 8 OFF)

- **On Shift Days (12-Hour Hospital Runs):**
  - High physical step count (10k–15k steps).
  - Pack portable liquid protein (Fairlife shakes), lactose-free milk drinks, and easy snacks.
  - Rely on cafeteria or deli choices with clear lean proteins (grilled chicken, turkey sandwich, egg scrambles).
  - Keep hydration high with water and electrolytes to prevent fatigue-driven snacking.
- **On Off Days (8-Day Rest & Recovery Stretch):**
  - Cook simple fresh meals or dine out socially without stress.
  - Distribute ~130g protein evenly across 3–4 meals.
